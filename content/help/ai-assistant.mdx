# AI assistant

The assistant drafts questions so you do not have to type every one.

## Two ways in

- **From the dashboard**: describe what you want in the **Create with AI** box. It starts a session and opens the assistant with your request.
- **Inside a session**: press **AI Assistant** in the session header to add to or rework what is already there.

## Nothing is saved until you save it

The assistant proposes a draft. For questions, press **Apply & save** to add them straight to your session. You can still change them in the [question editor](/help/question-editor) afterwards. If the draft replaces all questions in a session that already has votes, the button reads **Apply to editor** and loads it for review first, because saving would delete those votes. Session settings drafts load into the form and need **Save**. **Discard** throws a draft away.

## What it is good at

- Drafting a set of questions on a topic at a difficulty you choose.
- Rewriting existing questions: shorter, harder, a different tone.
- Answering questions about a past session, like how people voted or which quiz questions were hardest.

## Prompting tips

- Say the **topic**, the **audience** and **how many questions**: "6 questions on renewable energy for a non-technical audience" beats "make a quiz".
- For a quiz, say so, and it will mark a correct answer on every question.
- Ask for changes conversationally: "make questions 2 and 4 harder".

## Limits

Your plan may include an **AI allowance**. Check it on your [account page](/help/account-and-access). Lapsed accounts cannot use the assistant.
