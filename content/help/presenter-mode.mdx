# Presenter mode & shortcuts

Presenter mode is the full-screen view for the projector. It shows the join QR in the lobby, steps through questions, reveals results and gives you room controls.

## Controls

- **Next / Reveal answer / Show leaderboard / End quiz**: the main button advances the flow. Its label changes with the step.
- **Close entries / Open entries**: see [Managing the room](/help/managing-the-room).
- **Show join QR / Hide join QR**: toggles the corner QR in round-by-round quizzes.
- **Remove**: opens the participant drawer (quizzes and comment sessions).
- **Fullscreen**: also on **F**.

## Instagram badge

If you set an Instagram handle in your [account](/help/account-and-access), press **I** to show or hide a QR badge for it on the presenter screen.

<Callout type="warning">
If the session is inactive, the presenter shows a notice. Activate it in [session settings](/help/session-settings).
</Callout>
