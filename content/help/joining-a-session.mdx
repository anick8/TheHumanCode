# Joining a session

You do not need an account or an app.

1. **Scan the QR code** on the presenter's screen (or open the link they share).
2. Depending on the session, enter your **name** and/or **ID**. Polls do not ask for either.
3. Wait for the host. Your phone follows the presenter screen automatically.

## If you cannot get in

- **"Entries are closed"**: the host has stopped new people joining. Ask them to open entries.
- **"Wheel is full"**: the join limit has been reached.
- **"This session is closed"**: the session is inactive or the organizer's access has ended.

If you joined before entries closed, you can keep playing on the same device.

<Callout>
Your phone remembers you on that device. Leaving the page and coming back should put you back where you were.
</Callout>
