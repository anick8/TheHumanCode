# Create your first session

From the dashboard, create a session. You can also describe what you want in the **Create with AI** box and let the [AI assistant](/help/ai-assistant) start it for you.

## The form

- **Session Title**: only you see this.
- **URL Slug**: the address attendees open, `/vote/your-slug`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. **Generate New** makes a fresh one.
- **Session Type**: Poll, Quiz, Image & comments, Treasure Hunt or Wheel of Fortune. The type is **locked once the session has its first response** (a wheel locks after its first spin and unlocks again after a reset).
- **Require name / Require ID**: for sessions with named participants. At least one must stay on. See [Session settings](/help/session-settings).
- **Quiz format**: Classic or Round-by-round (quizzes only). See [Quiz](/help/quiz).
- **Join limit**: caps how many people can join a wheel (wheels only).
- **Results Display**: Live or After All Questions (polls only).
- **Active Session**: while off, attendees cannot open the session. Treasure Hunts hide every clue until you switch it on.

## Next steps

Save, then add questions in the [question editor](/help/question-editor). When you are ready, [run it live](/help/run-it-live).
