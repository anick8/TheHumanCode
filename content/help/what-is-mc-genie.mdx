# What is MC Genie?

MC Genie is a live audience-response tool. You, the **Organizer**, run a **Session** of questions. The room joins from their phones by scanning a QR code. Nobody needs an account or an app.

Your presenter screen sets the pace. It decides when the next question appears and when results are shown, and every phone follows it in real time.

## Five session types

| Type | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| [Poll](/help/poll) | Anonymous voting with live or end-of-session results |
| [Quiz](/help/quiz) | Scored questions with a leaderboard or round-by-round boards |
| [Image & comments](/help/comments) | Collecting reactions to images you upload |
| [Treasure Hunt](/help/treasure-hunt) | Printed QR clues hidden around a venue |
| [Wheel of Fortune](/help/wheel-of-fortune) | Random draws from people who join and entries you add |

## The basic flow

1. [Create a session](/help/create-your-first-session) and add questions (or let the [AI assistant](/help/ai-assistant) draft them).
2. Optionally [design it](/help/design-your-session) to match your event.
3. [Run it live](/help/run-it-live) from presenter mode.
4. Review and export [results](/help/results-and-export).
