# Results & export

Open a session's **Results** page to review what happened.

## Tabs

- **Overview**: totals for the session.
- **Questions**: per-question results.
- **Comments** (comment sessions) and **Spins** (wheels): the wall of comments or the history of spins.
- **Participants**: who took part (named sessions).
- **Scores** (Classic quizzes): the final ranking. Round-by-round quizzes have no cumulative scores.
- **Export & Share**: download your data or share the results.

## Export

From **Export & Share** you can download:

- Results as **JSON**.
- Results as **CSV**.
- Participant responses as **CSV**.

Files are named with the session slug and today's date.

<Callout type="warning">
[Clearing responses](/help/session-settings) removes this data. Export first.
</Callout>
