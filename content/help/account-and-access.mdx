# Account, plan & access

Open the avatar menu in the dashboard and choose **Account settings**.

## What is on the page

- **Profile**: display name and your Instagram handle (used for the presenter [badge](/help/presenter-mode)).
- **Plan**: your access status and the period it covers.
- **AI allowance**: how much of your [AI assistant](/help/ai-assistant) allowance you have used. A blank allowance means unlimited.
- **Security**: change your password, or sign out everywhere.
- **Data**: export your data.
- **Activity**: stats about your sessions.

## Access states

Access comes from a **Grant** with a date range.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Everything works |
| Upcoming | The grant has not started yet |
| Expired | Past its end date. A short grace period lets a live event finish |
| Revoked | Ended immediately by an administrator |

Signing in without a grant shows a **pending** page. Ask the person who runs your account to grant you access.

## When access ends

A **lapsed** organizer is read-only. You can sign in and view your sessions and results, but cannot create, edit, present, upload or use the AI assistant. Attendees see "This session is closed". Nothing is deleted, and renewing restores everything.
