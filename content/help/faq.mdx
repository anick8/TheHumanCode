# FAQ & troubleshooting

## Participants

### Do attendees need an account?
No. They scan the QR code and join.

### Someone cannot join.
Check that the session is **Active**, that entries are **open**, and (for a wheel) that the join limit is not reached. See [Managing the room](/help/managing-the-room).

### Can people change their vote or answer?
No. Votes and locked quiz answers are final.

## Organizers

### Why can I not change the session type?
It is locked after the first response. [Clear responses](/help/session-settings) to unlock it.

### Why can I not edit anything?
Your access may have lapsed. Lapsed accounts are read-only. See [Account, plan & access](/help/account-and-access).

### The AI assistant is not responding.
You may have used your AI allowance or hit the hourly limit. Check your [account page](/help/account-and-access).

### My Treasure Hunt QR shows a status message.
The session is inactive, or the clue was deleted. Activate the session. A deleted clue's code stays invalid.

### Are results kept after the event?
Yes, until you clear them. [Export](/help/results-and-export) anything you want to keep.
