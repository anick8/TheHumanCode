# Wheel of Fortune

A random draw. People join with a name and land on the wheel automatically, and you can add manual **Entries** yourself. You spin from the presenter screen and the server chooses the result.

## Entries

- **Joined** entries are created when a participant joins (name required, up to 24 characters, duplicates allowed).
- **Manual** entries are text you type, one at a time or pasted one per line.
- Removed entries stay listed greyed out and can be **restored**. There is no cap on entries.

## Spinning

1. You need at least 2 active entries.
2. Press **Space** (or the Spin button). The server picks uniformly at random.
3. Resolve the **Pick**: press **R** to remove it (a raffle) or **K** to keep it (repeat picks allowed).

Phones only show the latest Pick and whether it is theirs, never the entry list.

## Join limit and reset

- **Join limit**: caps how many participants can join. When the wheel is full the join screen says so. Manual entries do not count. You can raise or clear the limit any time.
- **Close entries**: stops new people joining, but you can still add manual entries.
- **Reset**: clears the spin history and restores all removed entries, keeping the joined participants so the same room can play again.

See all wheel keys in [Presenter mode & shortcuts](/help/presenter-mode). Spins are listed in the **Spins** tab of [results](/help/results-and-export).
