# Poll

A Poll collects anonymous votes and shows aggregate results. Polls are never scored and have no join step: attendees scan and vote.

## How it works

- Add questions with options. Attendees pick one option per question.
- Votes are anonymous and cannot be changed after they are submitted.
- **Results Display** controls when results appear:
  - **Live Results**: a results page shows after each question.
  - **After All Questions**: results are shown once, after the last question.
  You can change this later.

## Closing voting

Because a poll has no join step, **Close entries** closes voting for everyone. You can reopen at any time. See [Managing the room](/help/managing-the-room).

## Results

Totals per option appear in the results view and can be exported. See [Results & export](/help/results-and-export).
