# Managing the room

## Close entries

**Close entries** stops new people getting in. It is a manual toggle, never automatic, and you can **Open entries** again at any time.

- **Quiz, comments, wheel**: nobody new can join. A device that already joined keeps playing, and the join screen says entries are closed. On a wheel you can still add manual entries.
- **Poll**: there is no join step, so closing entries closes voting for everyone.

## Join limit (wheel)

An optional cap on how many participants can join a wheel. Once it is reached the join screen says the wheel is full and the presenter shows "Wheel is full". Manual entries never count. See [Wheel of Fortune](/help/wheel-of-fortune).

## Remove participant

In quizzes and comment sessions, open the participant drawer on the presenter screen and press **Remove** next to someone.

- It is a hard delete: their answers or comments go with them and the leaderboard re-ranks.
- They may rejoin while entries are open.
- Polls have nobody to remove, since votes are anonymous.
