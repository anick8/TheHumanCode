# Run it live

## Before the event

1. Make sure the session is **Active**.
2. Open the presenter screen from the dashboard (select a session and press **P**, or use the present button).
3. Check how it looks with [Design](/help/design-your-session) previews.

## Presenting

The presenter screen opens on a **lobby** with the join QR code. Put it on the projector. Participants scan it and land on the [join screen](/help/joining-a-session).

Use the main button (or the right arrow key) to move through the session. What each step does depends on the type:

- **Poll**: show a question, then its results.
- **Quiz**: open a question, **Reveal answer**, **Show leaderboard**, then **Next question** and finally **End quiz**.
- **Image & comments**: show each image and **Next image**.
- **Wheel**: spin with the space bar. See [Wheel of Fortune](/help/wheel-of-fortune).

Treasure Hunts have no presenter screen. Print the clue QR codes and, for a timed game, run it from the **Run hunt** page: approve players, start the clock and watch the leaderboard. See [Treasure Hunt](/help/treasure-hunt).

<Callout>
Press **F** for fullscreen. All shortcuts are in [Presenter mode & shortcuts](/help/presenter-mode).
</Callout>

## During and after

- [Close entries](/help/managing-the-room) when you do not want more people joining.
- Review [results and export](/help/results-and-export) afterwards. Results are kept until you clear them.
