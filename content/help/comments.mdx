# Image & comments

Upload images and let named participants react with free-text comments. Comment sessions are never scored.

## How it works

1. Add images (and optional prompts) in the [question editor](/help/question-editor).
2. Participants join with a name and/or ID. See [Session settings](/help/session-settings).
3. You show each image on the presenter screen and press **Next image**. The presenter shows a comment wall.

## Moderating

- Delete a single comment from the presenter or results view.
- [Remove a participant](/help/managing-the-room) to delete them and all their comments. They may rejoin while entries are open.
- [Close entries](/help/managing-the-room) to stop new people from joining. Anyone already in can keep commenting.

Comments appear in the **Comments** tab of [results](/help/results-and-export).
