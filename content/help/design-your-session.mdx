# Design your session

Brand a session so phones and the presenter screen match your event. Open the session's **Design** page.

## Look gallery

Pick a preset in one click:

<PresetSwatches />

You can also start from **Current** (what the session uses now) or **Custom**.

## Fine-tune

Adjust individual values:

- **Colours**: Primary, Background, Text and Cards.
- **Fonts**: a heading font and a body font.
- **Logo**: upload a logo shown on the session.

The panel warns you when your colour choices have poor contrast, so text stays readable on a projector.

## Previews

Preview tabs show how each screen looks for your session type, before you save. Press the sticky **Save** bar when you are happy.

<Callout>
Design only affects the session's participant and presenter screens. The dashboard keeps its own look.
</Callout>
