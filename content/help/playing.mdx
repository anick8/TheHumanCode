# Playing along

What you do depends on the session type. The host's screen sets the pace.

## Poll

Pick an option. Your vote is anonymous and cannot be changed once submitted. Results appear when the host moves on.

## Quiz

1. Choose an option. You can change it freely.
2. Press **Lock** to commit. Once locked it is final.
3. Locks close when the time runs out.
4. When the host reveals the answer, you see whether you were right.

Ties are broken by total response time, so quicker is better. In round-by-round quizzes only people who got a question right appear on its board.

## Image & comments

Write a comment on the current image. Your comment is shown under your name or ID.

## Wheel of Fortune

You are on the wheel as soon as you join. When the host spins, your phone shows the latest Pick and whether it is you.

## Treasure Hunt

Scan any clue's QR code to read it. Clues can be found in any order.

If the hunt is a game, you join first: enter your name and wait for the organizer to let you in. After the organizer starts the clock, each clue you scan counts, and your first time on each clue is kept. If a clue has choice buttons, tap one to reveal the next hint. Your first pick is locked for good, even if you reload or switch phones. You see your own count and the clock; your rank appears when the hunt stops.

If the hunt is not a game, there is no join step and nothing is recorded about you. Your choice locks on your phone only.

Questions? Ask the event organizer, or see the [FAQ](/help/faq).
