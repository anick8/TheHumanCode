# Session settings

Open a session to change its settings at any time, subject to the locks below.

## Results display (polls)

- **Live Results**: results appear after each question.
- **After All Questions**: results appear once, at the end.

## Identity

For quizzes and comment sessions, choose what participants must give:

- **Require name**: they enter a name before taking part.
- **Require ID**: an external ID such as an employee number or email. It recognises a returning participant.

At least one must be on. Polls are anonymous, and Treasure Hunts, wheels and Pictionary do not use these settings (Pictionary always asks for a name).

## Pictionary settings

- **Rounds**: how many rounds the game lasts (1 to 50, default 8).
- **Round time limit**: seconds the drawer has once they pick a word (10 to 600, default 80).
- **Word list** (the **Words** tab): one word or phrase per line, up to 40 characters each and 200 words. Duplicates are dropped. You need at least 3 words, because each round offers the drawer three to choose from; the editor warns you below that.

## What gets locked

- **Session type** is locked after the first response (a Pictionary game locks after its first round, and its word list locks with it).
- **Identity** options lock once there are responses.
- **Quiz format** is locked after the first answer.
- A question's **time limit** is frozen once it has answers.

## Active status

Turn **Active Session** off to stop attendees opening the session. Re-enable it any time.

## Clear responses

**Clear** deletes everything the audience produced (participants, answers, comments, spins) and keeps everything you wrote. It also unlocks the type and format settings again. Use it to reset a session between runs.

<Callout type="warning">
Clearing cannot be undone. [Export your results](/help/results-and-export) first if you need them.
</Callout>
