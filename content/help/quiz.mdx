# Quiz

A Quiz asks named participants questions that each have one correct option. Every quiz is scored and **host-paced**: everyone follows your presenter screen.

## The flow of one question

1. You open the question. A countdown starts.
2. Participants choose an option and **Lock** it. Until they lock, they can change their mind. After locking, the answer is final.
3. You press **Reveal answer**. Every locked answer is judged, points are awarded and the correct option shows on every device. Nothing about correctness is visible before the reveal.
4. You show the leaderboard (Classic) or the question board (Round-by-round), then move on.

## Time limit and points

- **Time limit**: from 5 to 120 seconds per question, 20 by default. Locks after the limit are refused (a one-second grace covers network delay). The limit is frozen once the question has answers.
- **Points**: set per question in the [question editor](/help/question-editor).

## Two formats

Choose when you create the quiz or in settings. The format is locked once the quiz has its first answer.

### Classic

Scores add up. A **Leaderboard** is shown between questions and as the final ranking. When you close a Classic quiz, the final results show an empty podium: press **→** (or **Reveal next place**) to unveil 3rd, then 2nd, then the winner. Each participant sees their own finish, the podium and their answers on their phone.

**Ties** are broken by cumulative **response time**: the faster total wins. Speed never changes the score, and exactly equal scores and times share a rank.

### Round-by-round

There is no running total. After each reveal, the **Question board** shows only the participants who got that question right, fastest first. The presenter shows the top 10 plus "+N more got it right". Phones show everyone, with their own row highlighted.

The quiz ends with a **Round recap**: each question with its fastest correct participant. Because nothing accumulates, people can **join mid-game** while entries are open. A small join QR stays in the corner of the presenter screen (you can hide it).

<Callout type="warning">
A participant who joins late in a Classic quiz starts as if they missed every question already revealed.
</Callout>

## Managing people

Use [Close entries and Remove participant](/help/managing-the-room) to control the room. Quizzes created before every quiz was scored may show a **Legacy** badge and keep their old scoring.
